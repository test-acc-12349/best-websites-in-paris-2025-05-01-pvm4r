Frequently Asked Questions

What is your typical website development timeline?

Most projects are completed within 4-8 weeks, depending on complexity. We follow a structured process: 1 week for planning, 2-3 weeks for design, 2-3 weeks for development, and 1 week for testing and launch.

What maintenance and support services do you offer?

We provide comprehensive support packages including regular updates, security monitoring, performance optimization, and content updates. Our team is available 24/7 for urgent issues.

How do you handle website security?

Security is our top priority. We implement SSL certificates, regular security audits, automated backups, and advanced firewall protection. All our sites comply with GDPR requirements.

What is included in your SEO optimization?

Our SEO package includes keyword research, on-page optimization, meta tag optimization, site speed improvements, and mobile optimization. We also provide monthly performance reports.

Do you offer custom design services?

Yes, all our designs are custom-created to match your brand identity. We work closely with you to understand your vision and create unique, engaging designs that stand out.
